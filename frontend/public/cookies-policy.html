What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on Your device when You visit a website. They help websites remember information about Your visit to improve functionality, security, and user experience.

How We Use Cookies

MultiCV uses cookies to enhance Your experience, analyze site usage, and manage user sessions. The cookies we use may include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Functional Cookies: Store user preferences, such as language selection.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through Your browser settings. Most browsers allow You to block or delete cookies, but this may affect site functionality.

To disable cookies, follow these links for popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Internet Explorer

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services such as Google Analytics to track user activity. These services may set their own cookies, which are subject to their respective privacy policies.

For more information about Google Analytics cookies, visit Google’s Cookie Policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Last updated" date. If necessary, We will request Your consent again.
